Privacy & support

SignalWatch is developed by Leo. Contact leolinnz@gmail.com with privacy, support or data deletion requests. Effective 23 October 2026.

What the watch app does

SignalWatch displays headlines and summaries from your chosen Signal server and lets you save stories and mark them as read. Demo mode uses sample content stored in the app and does not contact the Signal server.

Sign-in and account information

Live mode requires an existing Signal account. You sign in on your phone and explicitly approve a short-lived watch code. The watch does not ask for or store your password. After approval, it receives a server-issued account session cookie. The cookie is encrypted on the watch using a device-protected Android Keystore key. Account sessions identify the approving Signal account and allow access to its briefing, saved stories and read status.

Data sent to your server

The app connects over HTTPS to the server you select. Requests include account-session credentials, temporary pairing codes or device secrets, and the article links and bookmark/read actions you choose. The server may also process your account email and user identifier, network address, request timestamps and operational logs needed to run and protect the service. Bookmarks, read status and existing account information are stored on the Signal server. The watch stores the server address and encrypted session locally. It does not store a permanent article history.

Permissions and third parties

This release requests internet access. It has no advertising or analytics SDK, makes no in-app purchases, and requests no location, health, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. QR codes are generated locally. The phone's own camera can scan a code; SignalWatch does not access that camera. Network infrastructure and the hosting provider process traffic needed to deliver the service. The default service uses Tailscale Funnel for HTTPS hosting. If you select another Signal server, its operator and privacy practices may differ. Any Google sign-in occurs on the Signal website, under the policies of that service and Google.

Retention and your choices

Pairing codes expire after ten minutes or are consumed once. Server restarts invalidate outstanding codes. Sign-out clears the watch's saved session; uninstalling removes its app data. Neither action deletes your server account. Signal's server retains account information while the account exists; unbookmarked story history follows the server's configured retention period and saved stories remain until removed. Operational backups are retained separately and may keep previously stored information until rotated; ask the operator about the applicable backup schedule.

Request access or deletion

For the default service, email leolinnz@gmail.com from your account address and describe your request. We may verify account ownership before removing account data. Do not send your password. For a custom server, contact its operator. See the data request instructions.

Changes

Material changes to the app's data handling will be reflected in this page and the in-app privacy information.
