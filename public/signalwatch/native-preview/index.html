  • Continue listening & volume (0.14.0): resume your unfinished queue, story and saved speech section after closing the app, the paused-session timeout or restarting the watch. Resume listening appears in the briefing and Resume replaces Catch up on the tile while a position is saved. A tap is always required; Stop retains progress, finishing clears it, and Forget saved position removes it. Saved text supports offline watch speech. Changed loaded story text invalidates its old position. Volume down/up in the player adjust the current media output one step at a time, with its actual percentage and limits. Volume changes do not start playback or change alarm/notification settings. Fixed-volume outputs use their device controls. The existing companion remains compatible.
  • Catch me up (0.13.0): tap Catch me up in the briefing or Catch up on the tile for up to five new or changed unread stories, in personalised feed order, targeting about two minutes at 1x. Similar headlines are grouped into one attributed extract. Completed segments stay out of the next catch-up until their story text changes; skipping leaves them eligible. Save, Hear more and Open on phone are available in the player. Replay last catch-up works offline with its preparation time shown. Refresh first for current news. Companion 0.12.0 (21) remains compatible. Try the sample catch-up preview.
  • Wrist actions, AI takeaways, translation, markets & social (0.12.0): watch news notifications now include Listen, Save and Mark read quick actions, and automatically update the briefing tile and watch-face complication when a permitted push arrives. Audio playback adds speaking speed control (0.9x–1.5x), full-story section skipping and Shuffle unread. Open any story for AI key takeaways and one-tap Chinese/English translation. Check Markets & clocks for followed market tickers and your synced world clock, view live nearby hazards and a 10-alert watch disaster history, and share stories, comment or reply to friends' direct messages from your wrist.
  • Prominent disaster warnings: a new validated report opens a red warning immediately while SignalWatch is open and pauses narration. Acknowledge returns to your previous screen and keeps the report in history. In the background, disaster notifications have a warning icon, View alert action and a distinctive three-pulse vibration when allowed by the watch settings. Existing muted or disabled alert settings are preserved. Background alerts do not force the app open or bypass Do Not Disturb, and screen wake-up depends on Wear OS. Try the sample warning preview. Companion 0.12.0 (21) also includes local weather.
  • Local weather: open Local weather on the watch or in Signal Companion and allow approximate location. See current temperature, feels-like, humidity, wind and a seven-day forecast. Refresh location after travelling. Requests stop when weather leaves the foreground. Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo and does not change disaster-alert location.
  • Screen-off audio: listen while the display sleeps or another app is open, with system play/pause, previous, next and stop controls. Headphone disconnection or another audio app pauses playback.

  • Watch-face complication: add Signal unread to a compatible short- or long-text slot. See the last refreshed unread count and tap to open your briefing.
  • Voice search: tap Voice search and say a topic or keyword. Searches the loaded briefing or latest 30 saved stories. Cancel leaves your search unchanged; typing remains available.
  • Quick briefing tile: add Signal briefing in the watch tile picker. See the latest loaded headline, unread count and load time, then choose Open, Resume when a listening position is saved, or Catch up otherwise. Open SignalWatch and refresh for current news.

In Signal on your phone, enable Disaster alerts and Phone notifications & app badge, select hazards, radius and severity, and refresh the phone location at least daily. Link that same browser when approving Signal Companion. Disaster reports use these phone settings; weather location does not update them. Disaster reports bypass news filters, quiet hours and the news limit; Android notification settings still apply. Open Disaster alerts on the watch to review its last received report.

Reports may be delayed or incomplete. No alert does not mean your area is safe. Follow local authorities.

Enable alerts

  1. Install Signal Companion on the Android phone. Choose Connect phone, approve the code in the browser that currently receives this phone's Signal alerts, then enable notifications.
  2. Sign in to the same account on SignalWatch. Open Privacy & support, choose Enable watch notifications and allow notification permission.
  3. Keep phone and watch connected through Wear OS. Choose Send test notification in the phone companion.

Your phone keeps its native news alert. The companion asks the watch to display a native alert. A displayed watch alert or an intentional wrist filter makes that phone alert local-only. Filters do not remove the phone alert. If the watch is absent or cannot display it, normal phone forwarding remains available under your Wear OS notification settings.

Only the browser subscription explicitly linked during phone approval is replaced, and only after the companion confirms that Android posted its alert. Other devices keep their existing delivery. Disaster reports also reach the companion linked to the originating browser; browser disaster delivery remains active. Keep browser notifications subscribed for fallback. Delivery is best effort; receipt timeouts can cause a duplicate and can add up to twelve seconds before browser fallback.

Notification privacy

Optional notifications request Android notification permission. The companion uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, which processes an installation identifier, its messaging token and notification contents to deliver alerts. The Signal server stores that installation's token, account association, optional linked browser endpoint and a seven-day registration lease. Permitted deliveries renew the lease. Sign-out or disabling native alerts unregisters when connected; an expired or uninstalled installation cannot replace browser delivery.

The watch receives the alert through Google's signed Wear OS data layer and checks the Signal server, account fingerprint, local sign-in, permission and message deadline before posting. It stores an encrypted account fingerprint and verification time. Account sessions and companion configuration are encrypted with Android Keystore and excluded from backups. The watch stores its saved listening queue text and section position, its last catch-up and a bounded history of completed story versions, downloaded stories, up to ten supported full articles, pending bookmark/read actions, its tile/complication briefing and the latest received disaster report encrypted on the device. Downloads and pending actions are tied to the same server/account and cleared on sign-out. Voice search opens the system speech recognizer; its provider processes speech according to its own settings. Neither app contains an advertising or analytics SDK or requests health, contacts, camera or microphone access. Watch weather optionally uses precise or approximate foreground location; the current phone companion uses approximate location. Coordinates are sent directly to Open-Meteo; no background tracking or location history is stored. Existing website disaster alerts use the website's separate location permissions.

Sign-out clears the local account session. Uninstalling removes local app data. Neither deletes your Signal account; account deletion also removes its server-side notification installations.
