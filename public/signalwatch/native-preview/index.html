New watch features

  • Prominent disaster warnings: a new validated report opens a red warning immediately while SignalWatch is open and pauses narration. Acknowledge returns to your previous screen and keeps the report in history. In the background, disaster notifications have a warning icon, View alert action and a distinctive three-pulse vibration when allowed by the watch settings. Existing muted or disabled alert settings are preserved. Background alerts do not force the app open or bypass Do Not Disturb, and screen wake-up depends on Wear OS. Try the sample warning preview. The existing companion 0.8.0 (12) remains compatible.
  • Screen-off audio: listen while the display sleeps or another app is open, with system play/pause, previous, next and stop controls. Headphone disconnection or another audio app pauses playback.
  • Offline briefing: headlines and summaries stay on the watch. Choose Download full stories for up to ten supported articles. Read and bookmark changes queue offline and sync when connected. Offline listening uses an installed watch voice.
  • Watch-face complication: add Signal unread to a compatible short- or long-text slot. See the last refreshed unread count and tap to open your briefing.
  • Voice search: tap Voice search and say a topic or keyword. Searches the loaded briefing or latest 30 saved stories. Cancel leaves your search unchanged; typing remains available.
  • Quick briefing tile: add Signal briefing in the watch tile picker. See the latest loaded headline, unread count and load time, then choose Open or Listen. Open SignalWatch and refresh for current news.
  • Selective news: open Watch alerts. Choose topics or comma-separated keywords; a match to either qualifies. Empty filters select all news. Topic and keyword rules sync across this account's watches; quiet hours and the daily notification limit apply locally. Tap Save alert settings.
  • Disaster reports: turn Disasters on in Watch alerts and save. In Signal on your phone, enable Disaster alerts and Phone notifications & app badge, select hazards, radius and severity, and refresh the phone location at least daily. Link that same browser when approving Signal Companion. The watch uses these phone settings and requests no location permission. Disaster reports bypass news filters, quiet hours and the news limit; Android notification settings still apply. Open Disaster alerts on the watch to review its last received report.

Reports may be delayed or incomplete. No alert does not mean your area is safe. Follow local authorities.

Enable alerts

  1. Install Signal Companion on the Android phone. Choose Connect phone, approve the code in the browser that currently receives this phone's Signal alerts, then enable notifications.
  2. Sign in to the same account on SignalWatch. Open Privacy & support, choose Enable watch notifications and allow notification permission.
  3. Keep phone and watch connected through Wear OS. Choose Send test notification in the phone companion.

Your phone keeps its native news alert. The companion asks the watch to display a native alert. A displayed watch alert or an intentional wrist filter makes that phone alert local-only. Filters do not remove the phone alert. If the watch is absent or cannot display it, normal phone forwarding remains available under your Wear OS notification settings.

Only the browser subscription explicitly linked during phone approval is replaced, and only after the companion confirms that Android posted its alert. Other devices keep their existing delivery. Disaster reports also reach the companion linked to the originating browser; browser disaster delivery remains active. Keep browser notifications subscribed for fallback. Delivery is best effort; receipt timeouts can cause a duplicate and can add up to twelve seconds before browser fallback.

Notification privacy

Optional notifications request Android notification permission. The companion uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, which processes an installation identifier, its messaging token and notification contents to deliver alerts. The Signal server stores that installation's token, account association, optional linked browser endpoint and a seven-day registration lease. Permitted deliveries renew the lease. Sign-out or disabling native alerts unregisters when connected; an expired or uninstalled installation cannot replace browser delivery.

The watch receives the alert through Google's signed Wear OS data layer and checks the Signal server, account fingerprint, local sign-in, permission and message deadline before posting. It stores an encrypted account fingerprint and verification time. Account sessions and companion configuration are encrypted with Android Keystore and excluded from backups. The watch stores downloaded stories, up to ten supported full articles, pending bookmark/read actions, its tile/complication briefing and the latest received disaster report encrypted on the device. Downloads and pending actions are tied to the same server/account and cleared on sign-out. Voice search opens the system speech recognizer; its provider processes speech according to its own settings. Neither app contains an advertising or analytics SDK or directly requests location, health, contacts, camera or microphone access. Existing website disaster alerts use the website's separate location permissions.

Sign-out clears the local account session. Uninstalling removes local app data. Neither deletes your Signal account; account deletion also removes its server-side notification installations.
