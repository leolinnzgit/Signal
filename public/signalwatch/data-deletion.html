SIGNALWATCH

Account & data requests

For SignalWatch and the default Signal service operated by Leo, you can request account deletion, deletion of associated story bookmarks/read status, or access to your stored information.

  1. Email leolinnz@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Signal account.
  2. Use the subject SignalWatch account deletion. State whether you want the whole Signal account and associated data removed, or a specific data request.
  3. We will verify ownership before processing a deletion request. Do not include your password or watch session credentials.
Start an email request

Account deletion affects your Signal account on other devices too. Signing out of SignalWatch only clears the watch's local session and does not delete server data. Data may remain in existing operational backups until they rotate. Contact support about any applicable backup retention.

If you configured another Signal server, contact that server's operator instead; Leo cannot delete data stored on a server he does not operate.
