Enable alerts

  1. Install Signal Companion on the Android phone. Choose Connect phone, approve the code in the browser that currently receives this phone's Signal alerts, then enable notifications.
  2. Sign in to the same account on SignalWatch. Open Privacy & support, choose Enable watch notifications and allow notification permission.
  3. Keep phone and watch connected through Wear OS. Choose Send test notification in the phone companion.

Your phone keeps its native news alert. The companion asks the watch to display a native alert. Only a watch display acknowledgement makes that phone alert local-only. If the watch is absent or cannot display it, normal phone forwarding remains available under your Wear OS notification settings.

Only the browser subscription explicitly linked during phone approval is replaced, and only after the companion confirms that Android posted its alert. Other devices and location-based disaster alerts keep their existing delivery. Keep browser notifications subscribed for fallback. Delivery is best effort; receipt timeouts can cause a duplicate and can add up to twelve seconds before browser fallback.

Notification privacy

Optional notifications request Android notification permission. The companion uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, which processes an installation identifier, its messaging token and notification contents to deliver alerts. The Signal server stores that installation's token, account association, optional linked browser endpoint and a seven-day registration lease. Permitted deliveries renew the lease. Sign-out or disabling native alerts unregisters when connected; an expired or uninstalled installation cannot replace browser delivery.

The watch receives the alert through Google's signed Wear OS data layer and checks the Signal server, account fingerprint, local sign-in, permission and message deadline before posting. It stores an encrypted account fingerprint and verification time. Account sessions and companion configuration are encrypted with Android Keystore and excluded from backups. Neither app contains an advertising or analytics SDK or requests location, health, contacts, camera or microphone access. Existing website disaster alerts use the website's separate location permissions.

Sign-out clears the local account session. Uninstalling removes local app data. Neither deletes your Signal account; account deletion also removes its server-side notification installations.
